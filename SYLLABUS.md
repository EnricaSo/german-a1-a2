# Complete German A1 + A2 Syllabus (20 Modules / 33 Weeks)

## Phase 1: CEFR A1 (Weeks 1–15 | ~1.5 Weeks per Module)

### Module 01: First Contact (`m01_first_contact`)
- **Topics:** Greetings, farewells, alphabet/spelling, numbers 0–100, countries, languages.
- **Grammar:** Personal pronouns (*ich, du, er/sie/es, wir, ihr, sie/Sie*); regular verb conjugation (*-e, -st, -t, -en, -t, -en*); *sein* and *haben*; W-questions (*Wer? Wie? Woher? Was?*).
- **Targets:** Oral: Introduce yourself and spell your name. | Essay: 40-word profile bio.

### Module 02: Everyday Objects & Family (`m02_objects_family`)
- **Topics:** Family members, classroom/office items, colors, basic adjectives.
- **Grammar:** Grammatical gender (*der, die, das*); definite & indefinite articles (*ein, eine*); plural formation rules; negation (*nicht* vs. *kein/keine*).
- **Targets:** Oral: Describe your family and items on your desk. | Essay: 50-word family introduction.

### Module 03: Free Time & Hobbies (`m03_free_time_hobbies`)
- **Topics:** Sports, hobbies, days of the week, times of day, frequency adverbs (*oft, manchmal, nie*).
- **Grammar:** Stem-changing verbs (*fahren, lesen, sprechen, sehen*); main clause word order (Verb in Position 2, inversion after time expressions); Yes/No questions.
- **Targets:** Oral: Discuss weekend plans with a friend. | Essay: 60-word description of your typical week.

### Module 04: Food & Shopping (`m04_food_shopping`)
- **Topics:** Groceries, meals, restaurant ordering, prices, packaging/quantities (*ein Kilo, eine Flasche*).
- **Grammar:** **Accusative case** (*den, einen, keinen*); *möchten* (would like); *es gibt* + Accusative; personal pronouns in Accusative (*mich, dich, ihn, sie, es*).
- **Targets:** Oral: Order a meal at a restaurant and pay the bill. | Essay: 60-word favorite meal & shopping list.

### Module 05: Daily Routine & Time (`m05_daily_routine_time`)
- **Topics:** Official & unofficial clock times, daily habits, household chores.
- **Grammar:** **Separable verbs** (*aufstehen, einkaufen, fernsehen, anrufen*); temporal prepositions (*am* + day, *um* + time, *von...bis*).
- **Targets:** Oral: Schedule an appointment by phone. | Essay: 70-word morning and evening routine.

### Module 06: City & Navigation (`m06_city_navigation`)
- **Topics:** Places in town, public transport, asking for/giving directions, ordinal numbers (*erste, zweite*).
- **Grammar:** **Modal verbs** (*können, müssen, wollen, dürfen*) + infinitive at the end; Imperative mood (*du*, *ihr*, *Sie* forms).
- **Targets:** Oral: Ask for and give directions to the train station. | Essay: 75-word neighborhood and commute description.

### Module 07: Housing & Living (`m07_housing_living`)
- **Topics:** Rooms, furniture, appliances, apartment hunting, rental ads.
- **Grammar:** **Dative case** (articles: *dem, der, dem, den + -n*); static local prepositions with Dative (*in, an, auf, unter, über, vor, hinter, neben, zwischen* answering *Wo?*).
- **Targets:** Oral: Call a landlord to ask about an apartment. | Essay: 80-word description of your current home or dream apartment.

### Module 08: Health & Body (`m08_health_body`)
- **Topics:** Body parts, common illnesses, symptoms, doctor's visit, pharmacy.
- **Grammar:** Modal verb *sollen* (advice/instructions); possessive articles (*mein, dein, sein, ihr, unser, euer*) in Nominative & Accusative; *weh tun* + Dative.
- **Targets:** Oral: Explain symptoms to a doctor. | Essay: 80-word sick-note email to an employer or teacher.

### Module 09: Past Events & Holidays (`m09_past_events_holidays`)
- **Topics:** Weather, seasons, months, vacation activities, travel memories.
- **Grammar:** **Perfekt (Conversational Past)** with *haben* and *sein* (regular *ge-...-t* and irregular *ge-...-en* participles); *Präteritum* of *sein* (*war*) and *haben* (*hatte*).
- **Targets:** Oral: Talk about what you did last weekend. | Essay: 90-word postcard or email about a past trip.

## Phase 2: CEFR A2 (Weeks 16–33 | ~1.5–2 Weeks per Module)

### Module 10: Biographies & Childhood (`m10_biographies_childhood`)
- **Topics:** Life stages, school subjects, childhood memories, historical milestones.
- **Grammar:** **Präteritum** of modal verbs (*musste, konnte, wollte, durfte, sollte*); temporal subordinate clauses with *als* (single past event) vs. *wenn* (repeated/present).
- **Targets:** Oral: Discuss rules from your childhood. | Essay: 100-word biography of a family member.

### Module 11: Work & Career (`m11_work_career`)
- **Topics:** Professions, office tasks, education, job interviews, CVs.
- **Grammar:** Subordinate clauses with **weil** (because) and **dass** (that) — conjugated verb at the very end; *deshalb* (therefore).
- **Targets:** Oral: Explain your job/studies and why you chose your field. | Essay: 100-word cover email for an internship/job.

### Module 12: Clothing & Appearance (`m12_clothing_appearance`)
- **Topics:** Clothing items, fabrics, sizes, shopping dialogues, compliments.
- **Grammar:** **Adjective declension** in Nominative and Accusative (after definite & indefinite articles); Comparative & Superlative (*groß, größer, am größten*; *gern, lieber, am liebsten*).
- **Targets:** Oral: Shop for clothes, asking for different sizes/colors. | Essay: 110-word comparison of two cities or shopping styles.

### Module 13: Relationships & Emotions (`m13_relationships_emotions`)
- **Topics:** Friendships, family dynamics, feelings, apologies, invitations/parties.
- **Grammar:** **Reflexive verbs** (*sich freuen, sich ärgern, sich treffen, sich interessieren für*); Dative personal pronouns (*mir, dir, ihm, ihr, uns, euch, ihnen*); Dative verbs (*helfen, danken, gefallen, gehören*).
- **Targets:** Oral: Apologize for being late and explain why. | Essay: 110-word description of a close friend.

### Module 14: Media & Technology (`m14_media_technology`)
- **Topics:** Smartphones, internet, news, TV/streaming habits, remote work.
- **Grammar:** Indirect questions (*Können Sie mir sagen, wann/ob...?*); verbs with fixed prepositions (*warten auf, denken an, sprechen über*) + *wo(r)- / da(r)-* compounds.
- **Targets:** Oral: Discuss your daily screen time and media habits. | Essay: 120-word pros and cons of working from home.

### Module 15: Travel & Hotels (`m15_travel_hotels`)
- **Topics:** Booking hotels, train travel, sightseeing, lodging complaints.
- **Grammar:** **Two-way prepositions (*Wechselpräpositionen*)**: Accusative for direction/movement (*Wohin?*) vs. Dative for static position (*Wo?*); positional verbs (*stehen/stellen, liegen/legen, sitzen/setzen*).
- **Targets:** Oral: Complain at a hotel reception about a noisy or dirty room. | Essay: 120-word review of a hotel or restaurant.

### Module 16: Environment & Nature (`m16_environment_nature`)
- **Topics:** Recycling, weather extremes, climate, city vs. countryside living.
- **Grammar:** **Konjunktiv II** for polite requests, advice, and hypothetical wishes (*könnte, würde + infinitive, hätte, wäre, sollte*).
- **Targets:** Oral: Give polite advice to a stressed friend. | Essay: 130-word essay on what you would do if you won the lottery or lived off-grid.

### Module 17: Culture & Traditions (`m17_culture_traditions`)
- **Topics:** Public holidays, festivals, customs, dining etiquette, invitations.
- **Grammar:** **Relative clauses** in Nominative and Accusative (*der Mann, der...*; *die Frau, die...*; *das Buch, das...*).
- **Targets:** Oral: Invite a colleague to dinner and coordinate dietary restrictions. | Essay: 130-word description of a traditional holiday in your country.

### Module 18: Sports & Fitness (`m18_sports_fitness`)
- **Topics:** Exercise routines, nutrition, team vs. individual sports, minor injuries.
- **Grammar:** **Infinitive clauses with *zu*** (*Ich habe keine Zeit, Sport zu treiben*); final clauses with *um...zu* (in order to) and *damit* (so that).
- **Targets:** Oral: Debate whether team sports or solo workouts are better. | Essay: 140-word practical guide for living a healthy lifestyle.

### Module 19: Banking & Bureaucracy (`m19_banking_bureaucracy`)
- **Topics:** Post office, opening a bank account, forms, online orders, returns/complaints.
- **Grammar:** **Passive voice** in Present tense (*wird gemacht / werden geliefert*); concessive connector *trotzdem* (nevertheless) vs. *obwohl* (although).
- **Targets:** Oral: Open a bank account or ship an international parcel. | Essay: 140-word formal complaint letter about a damaged online order.

### Module 20: A2 Consolidation & Exam Prep (`m20_consolidation_exam_prep`)
- **Topics:** Comprehensive review across all A1–A2 themes.
- **Grammar:** Full synthesis of Cases (Nom/Acc/Dat), Tenses (Präsens/Perfekt/Präteritum), Subordinate Clauses, and Adjective Endings.
- **Targets:** Oral: Full 3-part Goethe A2 speaking simulation. | Essay: 150-word formal + informal exam writing tasks.
