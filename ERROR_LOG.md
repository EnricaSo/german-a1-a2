# Error Log

| Date | Module | Category | Mistake | Correction | Rule / Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-16 | M01 | W-Questions | *Was ist dein Nachname?* | **Wie** ist dein Nachname? | Use **Wie** (not *Was*) for names, numbers, and addresses. |
| 2026-11-16 | M01 | Conjugation (`Sie`) | *specht Sie* / *buchstabiere Sie* | **sprechen** Sie / **buchstabieren** Sie | Formal **Sie** always takes the **-en** ending (infinitive form). Also watch spelling of *sprechen* (*spricht, sprach, hat gesprochen*). |
| 2026-11-16 | M01 | Possessives (`Sie`) | *Sein Nachname* (for formal "your") | **Ihr** (Accusative: *Ihren Nachnamen*) | Formal "your" is **Ihr** (capitalized); *sein* means "his". |
| 2026-11-16 | M01 | Numbers | *einsundzwanzig* | **einundzwanzig** | In compound numbers (21, 31, ...), *eins* drops `-s` → **ein-**. |
| 2026-11-16 | M01 | Orthography & Vocab | *Tchuss* / *spaete* ("tomorrow") | **Tschüss** / **morgen** (*später* = later) | Watch `Tsch-` and umlauts (`ü`, `ä`); *morgen* = tomorrow, *später* = later. |
